# Capture the process

The person does most of the job without thinking about it. The bot needs that knowledge written down. Get it in three passes: brain dump, play back, then real examples.

## 1. Brain dump

Ask them to talk, by voice if they can, without organizing. Tell them a messy dump is fine. Ask them to cover:

- the steps they take
- what they watch out for
- the apps and tools they use
- where the information comes from: email, chat, a ticket, a website, a phone call
- how they check the work was done right
- where they hear how it went: reviews, customers, coworkers

## 2. Play back

Restate the process in 5 to 8 plain steps. Mark each guess you made. Ask what is wrong or missing. Repeat until they say it is right. Do not start the work.

## 3. Judgment calls

Ask for three real cases, including one that went wrong or needed a different call. Walk each case through the played-back steps out loud. Then ask one question at a time from this list, skipping any already answered:

- Which source wins when two disagree? Example: the email says 3pm and the booking app says 4pm.
- What does done mean? Sent is not done. Saved, confirmed, and recorded is done.
- What changes the plan? Which jobs take longer, who can do what, when to leave extra time.
- What is still open, and how does the bot track it so a retry does not do the work twice?
- When should the bot ask the person instead of deciding?
- What must the bot never do?

Write each answer as a rule in the plan. Then ask: "What have I not asked about yet?"

## 4. Better way

Check the process against the goal. Name any step that does not serve the goal and any step a bot could do differently. Offer the change. Keep their way if they prefer it.

Done when the person confirms the steps and the rules.
